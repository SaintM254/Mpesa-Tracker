Everything you need to understand your money

Four core capabilities that turn a noisy SMS inbox into a calm, useful dashboard.

sms

Automatic M-PESA SMS parsing

M-PESA Tracker listens for M-PESA confirmation SMSes the moment they land in your inbox and extracts the merchant, amount, balance, and reference code. No manual entry, no copy-paste — every Till, Paybill and person-to-person transfer is captured and tagged the instant you receive it.

  • check_circle Recognises M-PESA, Airtel Money and T-Kash formats
  • check_circle Smart duplicate detection across retries
  • check_circle Works with double-SIM devices
category

Custom presets & categories

Pre-loaded with the categories Kenyans actually use — Food, Matatu, KPLC, Rent, School fees — and fully editable. Create your own rules so your regular KPLC paybill number always lands in Utilities.

palette

Material You dynamic interface

Dynamic colour, generous rounded corners and distinct surface tints adapt to your system theme. Switch between Light and Dark with one tap — both modes are first-class, contrast-checked and easy on the eyes.

Light Dark System
offline_bolt

100% offline, local storage

Everything is written directly to your phone's private app folder — no accounts, no analytics, no remote server. Your money history stays on your device, even on Airplane mode.

lock Requires RECEIVE_SMS permission only.

insights The dashboard

A summary that actually summarises

A glanceable home screen with monthly totals, week-over-week delta, and a categorised, colour-coded list of every M-PESA transaction — all parsed from SMS in real time.

  • restaurant

    Categorised by colour

    Each category — Food, Transport, Utilities, Airtime, Rent, Shopping, Savings — has a distinct, accessible colour in both Light and Dark mode.

  • savings

    Income vs expense

    Sent and received payments are split automatically. See your real net spend for the month, not just a long transaction list.

  • search

    Searchable history

    Find any transaction by merchant, amount or reference code — perfect for reconciling that one Till payment from last week.

Privacy by default

The app has no internet permission at all. It cannot upload your SMS even if it wanted to. Your transaction history lives in a private app folder only this APK can read.
